• NimHunt is a geofaucet-style mini-app for Nimiq. People can create NIM-funded geographic Spots, and other users can visit those locations to claim a reward.

  • NimHunt is designed to run inside Nimiq Pay. Public information can be viewed in a normal browser, but creating Spots and making claims requires Nimiq Pay to identify your device and handle payments.

  • Your location is used to show nearby Spots and to confirm that you are inside a Spot’s required radius when you claim. Keeping precise location enabled makes those checks more reliable.

  • Open Find Spots, choose a Spot, travel inside its radius and press Claim. Some Spots may also require you to remain there for a set time or enter a private code.

  • A Prizedraw Spot collects valid participants until it closes, then randomly selects the configured number of winners. Entering successfully does not guarantee that you will receive a prize.

  • Spot creators can protect claims with private one-use codes. The creator decides how those codes are distributed, so you will need to obtain one from them before claiming.
